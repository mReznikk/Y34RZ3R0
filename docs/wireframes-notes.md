# Year Zero longread — design decisions

## v2 · «Проявка» redesign (Oct 17, 2026) — current
The Figma file from v1 is superseded: the Figma Starter plan's MCP tool limit was reached.

**Concept.** Year Zero hid content under surfaces (toilet stall, shirt print, spectrogram, thermochromic coating, scratch layer). The page has an OFFICIAL layer (bone paper, reading column) and a LEAK layer (signal black), and each chapter has one moment where something surfaces because the reader acted. A heat-coloured wire runs down the left rail from the flash drive in the opening, touches every piece of evidence (11 total, counted bottom-left) and becomes the connection map in ch.4.

**Signature motion.** A: tour-shirt letters → I AM TRYING TO BELIEVE → iamtryingtobelieve.com (sticky stage). B: scratch-to-reveal Another Version of the Truth (touch must be armed first; toggle fallback). C: wire → connection map (nodes passed = solid, interacted = heat).
Supporting: H1 lines drift in opposite directions; chapter devices (01 aperture, 02 waveform letters flatten, 03 slipped lower half, 04 four strips align); pinned pauses for the two questions and the four readers; redaction bars retract; final sentence settles from scattered words. Reduced motion collapses every stage to its final state.

**System.** Display Alumni Sans 800/100 (Cyrillic), text PT Serif 19/31, utility IBM Plex Mono. Colours: black #0D0D0C, bone #E6E3DA, heat #FF4D1A (lines and bars only on bone), heat-hi #FFC861, cold #8FA6B8 / cold-ink #3E5A70 for the official voice. Imagery: documentary only, 1-bit dither; outline line-art for artifacts; Dymo strips for names and terms.

**Reference → use.** Linkin Park: indented cascades, cable → wire, title split around the disc (EXTERMINAL [CD] .NET). ABBA: pinned object, giant thin names + cut-out heads, device frame. Punk Rock: Dymo strips, vertical spine, inline ▶ cues, letter tiles. Fight Club: black pause screens, stepped lines, highlight bars. after-life: bracketed mono UI, sticky technical figure with callouts, «Рис.» captions, dithering. war.artmilitonian: timestamp block, opposing headline drift, line-art frieze, thin numerals, credit lines.

**Assets still needed.** Flash drive photo; real shirt print; Parepin screenshot; two voice portraits; real spectrogram frame; thermochromic CD scan; both AVOTT layers; audio rights; sources/credits.

**Copy kept verbatim for the editor.** «Перыая», «альбому??», «сделать чтобы», «проотиворечит», «СД» ×2, «восприятие восприятия», «В марте 2007 года,», «адрес - ещё».
