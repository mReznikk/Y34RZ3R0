# «Проявка» v4: change spec (26 Oct 2026)

Apply to the existing HTML prototype; keep content, order and wording.

1. **Colour:** the signal colour moves from red-orange to blue everywhere: tokens, canvas drawings, spectrogram ramp, CD glow, glitch overlays and the intro. Every text pair keeps 4.5:1 or better.
2. **Bureau loading screen:** shows on every load and refresh, about 5.5s. The four lines step through the run, and the progress bar still can't finish before the page is actually ready. «Skip intro» and Esc still work.
3. **Type:** Tektur (Google Fonts, Cyrillic, wght 400–900, wdth 75–100) replaces Roboto Flex for all display text.
4. **Three text levels only:** Heading (Tektur, one size scale), Body (IBM Plex Sans 18px), Small (IBM Plex Mono 11–12px). Lead sizes, hairline display, giant numerals and other in-between sizes fold into these three.
5. **Remove** the evidence counter in the bottom-left corner.
6. **Header:** one thin overall progress line only; no chapter segments.
7. **Glitch:** no glitch on text. Glitches happen only on specific scroll events (chapter change, a black↔paper section cut, the intro exit), made realistic in the spirit of Rob Sheridan's Year Zero visuals: real horizontal tearing of the page (SVG displacement), RGB channel offset, grid-aligned JPEG macroblocks with smear, interlace lines.
8. **Voices** (Capital G / The Good Soldier): brutalist redesign — hard rectangles, exposed rules, solid heavy names, flat portrait blocks; no placeholder borders or label chips; the caption sits under the block.
9. **Scratch module:** erasing removes layer A in rectangular, grid-snapped glitch segments with displaced strips instead of a round brush. The % counter, unlock and toggle stay.
10. **Redaction quote:** starts fully covered by black rectangles; the section pins while the bars clear word by word and unpins only after every bar is gone. Native scroll, no hijacking. Reduced motion shows the text uncovered.
