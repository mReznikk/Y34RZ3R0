# «Проявка» v5: change spec (26 Oct 2026)

Builds on v4. Article wording and order stay unchanged; added elements are marked «добавлено».

1. **Heading weight:** Tektur at one weight (500) and one width everywhere. Accent only by colour.
2. **Full-screen phrases:** the title, the two questions in chapter 01 and «Песня закончилась…» become pinned full-screen phrases; each word floats in with scroll.
3. **Chapter openers:** drop the numerals (01–04) and keep the titles. Once scrolled past, a title glitches out; scrolling back brings it back.
4. **After the Bureau screen:** the title page starts buried under moving static and heavy tearing. Scrolling clears the noise and reveals the title words.
5. **Footer:** credits and the view tools live in a footer that is hidden until the very end, then slides up.
6. **Spectrogram:** a longer pin. After the sweep, scrolling scrubs through a glitching frame sequence until the outline of the Presence appears. Real frames can be dropped in via `data-frames`.
7. **Horizontal section:** «pages you could pass through»: iamtryingtobelieve.com, exterminal.net, Another Version of the Truth, the forum. Pinned; vertical scroll moves it sideways.
8. **Redaction quote:** midway through clearing the bars, a short glitch flashes an «Art Is Resistance» image slot (no reproduction of the original emblem).
9. **Gesamtkunstwerk:** pinned sequence — placeholder blocks build up, then the word fills the screen, then the definition floats in word by word.

All pins use native scroll. Reduced motion shows final states without pins or noise.
